Solar &amp; Power

How to Calculate Solar Panel Wattage

By Shahanur Mollah · Published September 2026 · Updated October 2026
Quick answerPanel wattage is calculated from voltage and current at the same operating point: watts = volts × amps. The wattage printed on a panel is normally its rated maximum power under specified test conditions, not a guaranteed output in every real-world situation.

Worked panel example

  • If a panel operates at 40 V and 10 A at its maximum-power point, its calculated power is 40 × 10 = 400 W.
  • Do not multiply Voc by Isc and call that the panel's rated wattage; those are different test points.
  • Actual output changes with sunlight, panel temperature, orientation, shading, soiling, and system losses.
  • For system planning, use the panel's Pmax together with local solar conditions and the controller/inverter's electrical limits.

Use voltage and current

Panel power can be estimated as voltage multiplied by current at the panel's rated operating point. Manufacturers usually provide a maximum-power voltage and current.

Rated wattage is not constant output

Actual production changes with sunlight, temperature, shading, panel angle, dirt, wiring losses, and controller efficiency.

Use the specification label

For planning, record Voc, Isc, Vmp, Imp, and rated watts. Do not size a controller from wattage alone.

Estimate daily energy

Multiply the system's rated power by equivalent peak-sun hours, then apply realistic system losses. This is an estimate rather than a guarantee.

Design around the complete system

Panel wattage must be compatible with the controller, battery voltage, wiring, protection devices, and expected load.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Multiplying panel voltage and current from mismatched operating points.
  • Treating rated wattage as the exact output in every weather condition.
  • Using Voc and Isc as if they were normal operating voltage and current.
  • Ignoring shading, temperature, orientation, and controller losses when estimating daily energy.

Questions to check before you finish

How is panel wattage calculated?

At a particular operating point, power is approximately voltage × current. A panel's rated maximum power is based on its specified maximum-power voltage and current.

Why does a panel rarely produce its rated wattage?

Rated power is measured under standard test conditions. Sun angle, temperature, shading, dirt, wiring, and controller behaviour can reduce actual output.

Should I use Voc and Isc to calculate rated power?

No. Voc and Isc are open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current. Rated maximum power is based on the operating point Vmp × Imp.
