How to Check Laptop Battery Health

By Shahanur Mollah · Published September 2026 · Updated October 2026
Quick answerCheck laptop battery health using the operating system's built-in report when available, then compare the full-charge capacity with the original design capacity. Treat the result as a condition indicator rather than an exact prediction of remaining life.

What to look for in a battery report

  • Compare design capacity and full-charge capacity. A large difference indicates the battery no longer stores as much energy as when new.
  • Look for unusual charging behaviour, unexpected shutdowns, or rapid percentage drops that agree with the report.
  • Battery runtime also depends on screen brightness, processor load, wireless activity, and background software, so health percentage alone does not determine runtime.
  • If the battery is swollen, unusually hot, or physically damaged, stop using it and seek qualified service rather than continuing normal charging.

Windows battery report

Windows can generate a battery report that includes design capacity and recent battery information. The exact command and report location can vary by Windows version.

Compare design and full-charge capacity

A large gap between the original design capacity and the current full-charge capacity indicates wear, although the figures are estimates and can change with calibration.

Look for symptoms

Unexpected shutdowns, very short runtime, or a battery that drops rapidly under normal use can indicate wear.

Heat matters

Keep vents clear and avoid sustained high temperatures. Heat can accelerate battery ageing.

Replacement considerations

Use a compatible replacement from a reputable source and follow the laptop manufacturer's service guidance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Treating the design capacity as the same as the battery's current full-charge capacity.
  • Judging battery condition from one short runtime test.
  • Continuing to use a physically swollen or damaged battery.
  • Replacing the battery without checking the exact model, connector, voltage, and compatibility.

Questions to check before you finish

What does a battery report tell me?

It can show design capacity, recent full-charge capacity, usage history, and other information that helps compare current capacity with the original specification.

Does reduced capacity always mean the battery needs replacement?

Not necessarily. Battery age and reduced runtime are normal wear indicators. Replacement depends on capacity, runtime needs, physical condition, and the device's battery behaviour.

What if the battery is swollen?

Stop using and charging a visibly swollen or damaged battery and follow the manufacturer's service guidance. Do not puncture, crush, or attempt to open it.
