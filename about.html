01 · Background

Why I Started Building

"I created SMCyvera with a simple goal: to make technology easier to understand and everyday digital problems easier to solve."

Hello, I'm Shahanur Mollah, the creator and developer behind SMCyvera.

Like many people, I frequently encountered frustratingly vague technology advice online—generic listicles, automated checklists, and articles recommending expensive hardware upgrades without identifying the actual problem.

I built SMCyvera as a practical technology resource where readers can explore step-by-step guides, interactive troubleshooting solutions, and useful digital calculators. The platform focuses on five key areas: Android, Windows & PC hardware, Wi-Fi networking, solar power systems, and electronics.

As the developer and writer, my goal is not just to show what to do, but to explain why a solution works, verify the mathematical principles behind it, and be honest about its physical limits.

Core Belief: Technology should be understandable, useful, and accessible to everyone. Thank you for visiting SMCyvera and being part of this journey.

03 · Philosophy

How I Approach Every Solution

Three principles guide every tutorial, diagnostic checkpoint, and calculator published on SMCyvera.

01

Understand the problem.

Every malfunction has a root cause. Before deleting files, resetting network stacks, or altering system files, we isolate whether the issue is caused by software, settings, or failing hardware.

02

Explain the solution.

A step is only useful when you understand why it works. We show the reasoning, configuration parameters, and transparent mathematical formulas behind our recommendations.

03

Be honest about limitations.

Generic tips cannot repair swollen lithium batteries, water damage, or worn mechanical components. We clearly state when software troubleshooting stops and professional hardware replacement is needed.
