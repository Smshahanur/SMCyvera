Solar &amp; Power

How to Read a Solar Panel Specification Label

Published September 2026 · Practical technology guide
Quick answerRead a solar-panel label by identifying the maximum power (Pmax), voltage at maximum power (Vmp), current at maximum power (Imp), open-circuit voltage (Voc), and short-circuit current (Isc). These values describe different operating points and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Example of reading the label

  • If a label says Pmax 450 W, Vmp 41 V, and Imp 10.98 A, the product of Vmp × Imp is approximately 450 W, allowing for rounding.
  • Voc is higher than Vmp under standard test conditions, so controller or inverter PV-voltage checks should use the appropriate maximum voltage information rather than Pmax alone.
  • Isc is not the same as the normal operating current. It is measured under a short-circuit condition and is useful when checking electrical limits.
  • Also record the panel's temperature coefficient and dimensions when designing an array, because voltage and physical fit matter in real installations.

Maximum power

The rated maximum power is normally given in watts under defined test conditions.

Vmp and Imp

Maximum-power voltage and current are the operating values used to calculate rated power: approximately Vmp × Imp.

Voc and Isc

Open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current are important for controller and wiring limits. Voc can rise in cold conditions.

Series and parallel planning

Series connections increase voltage; parallel connections increase current for matching panels. The controller must remain within its PV limits.

Do not ignore protection

PV systems require appropriate cables, connectors, overcurrent protection where applicable, and safe isolation procedures.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Reading Voc as the normal operating voltage.
  • Comparing panel wattage without checking Vmp and Imp.
  • Ignoring temperature effects when assessing voltage limits.
  • Designing a series string without checking the controller's maximum PV voltage.

Questions to check before you finish

What is Vmp?

Vmp is the panel voltage at its rated maximum-power point. It is normally more useful for operating-point calculations than Voc.

What is Voc?

Voc is open-circuit voltage—the voltage measured when the panel is not supplying current. It is important when checking the maximum PV voltage of a controller or inverter.

Why do temperature and sunlight matter?

A panel's electrical output changes with conditions. Rated values are test-condition figures, so real voltage, current, and power can differ in the field.
