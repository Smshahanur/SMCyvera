Hardware Safety Protocol

Device Safety & Escalation Thresholds

Understand when an Android problem can be solved through software settings and when physical inspection is necessary.

Battery Safety

Swollen Batteries & Chemical Odors

If the rear glass is pushing outward, the screen bows, or the phone emits a sweet or pungent chemical smell, STOP charging immediately. Disconnect power at the wall outlet, power off if safe, and never compress or puncture the battery. Place on a non-flammable surface away from combustibles and take to an authorized technician or recycling center.

Port & Cable Safety

Burning Smells & Damaged Ports

If you smell burning plastic, observe smoke, or see scorch marks near the USB-C port, cut power from the wall outlet immediately. Never insert metal needles, pins, or paperclips into charging ports—conductive metal shorts power pins (VBUS/GND), causing sparks and destroying controller ICs. Use only non-conductive wooden/plastic picks gently, or dry compressed air.

Liquid Exposure

Moisture Alerts & Water Ingress

If your phone flags moisture in the charging port, do NOT connect a charging cable. Electrical current through wet contacts causes immediate electrolytic corrosion on connector pins. Stand the phone upright in front of a gentle room-temperature fan. Never use hair dryers, heat guns, ovens, or uncooked rice.

Thermal Limits

Operating Temperatures & Sensor Locations

Major manufacturers (Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi) specify normal operating ambient ranges between 0°C and 35°C (32°F to 95°F). Direct sunlight and warm car dashboards trap heat. Internal CPU junctions naturally reach higher temperatures under load, while battery thermistors trigger safety throttling if warm. Warm metal frames reflect normal heat dissipation.

Data Protection

Factory & Network Reset Warnings

Never perform a factory data reset without confirming your photos, messages, authenticator seeds, and contacts are synced to Google Drive or a PC. Network resets erase saved Wi-Fi passwords and paired Bluetooth accessories. Always ensure you know your Google account credentials to pass Factory Reset Protection (FRP).
