Battery Safety

Swelling & Heat Thresholds

If the rear glass is pushing outward, the screen flexes, or the phone emits a chemical smell, STOP charging immediately. Never puncture swollen cells. Bring the device to a qualified service technician.

Data Protection

Before Resetting or Servicing

Never perform a factory data reset without confirming your photos, two-factor authenticator seeds, and contacts are synced to Google Drive or an external PC backup.

Water Exposure

Moisture in USB-C Port

If your phone flags moisture in the charging port, do NOT plug in a cable. Stand the device upright in front of a fan. Charging with wet contacts triggers instant electrolytic corrosion on connector pins.
