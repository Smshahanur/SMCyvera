Privacy Policy — SMCyvera by Shahanur Mollah

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how SMCyvera (https://smcyvera.vercel.app/), created and developed by Shahanur Mollah, handles information, browser cookies, advertising, and user privacy choices.

1. Overview and Website Purpose

SMCyvera is an independent technology resource providing step-by-step troubleshooting explainers, educational guides, and browser-based calculators covering Android devices, Windows PCs, Wi-Fi networking, solar power systems, and electronics. We are committed to transparency, minimal data collection, and protecting your privacy.

2. Information You Provide and Browser Tools

Browsing SMCyvera's guides and using our interactive tools—including the Battery Runtime Calculator, Solar Energy Estimator, Inverter Load Calculator, Data Converter, and the 60-problem Android Troubleshooting Center—does not require creating an account, registering, or submitting personal information. All calculations and interactive diagnostic decision trees run locally and client-side within your browser.

If you contact the site creator via email or through the Contact page, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message contents) is used solely to evaluate your feedback, respond to inquiries, or review reported technical corrections. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email correspondence to third parties.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

SMCyvera uses Google AdSense to display advertisements that support the free publication of guides and tools. To provide relevant advertising and comply with Google AdSense program policies, the following disclosures apply:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to SMCyvera and/or other sites across the internet.
  • These advertising cookies allow Google to collect non-personally identifiable browsing data (such as browser type, time and date of visit, and subject of advertisements clicked or scrolled over) to measure ad performance and display relevant advertising.

4. Your Privacy Choices and Ad Opt-Outs

Visitors have clear choices regarding how their information and cookies are used for advertising:

  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising served by Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-Party Ad Opt-Out: You can opt out of many third-party advertising vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page at www.aboutads.info/choices or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Browser Cookie Controls: Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is placed. Note that disabling cookies will not impair the functionality of SMCyvera's calculators, diagnostic tools, or guides.

5. Third-Party Links

Articles and guides on SMCyvera may link to external websites, including manufacturer documentation, official software downloads, and technical standards bodies. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites they visit.

6. Data Security

We maintain reasonable technical safeguards to protect any direct communication received through the website. Since SMCyvera does not process financial transactions, store passwords, or host user accounts, we do not maintain databases of visitor personal data.

7. Policy Updates and Contact

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in site features, advertising practices, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit feedback, please reach out via our Contact page or email Shahanur Mollah directly at smcyvera@gmail.com.
