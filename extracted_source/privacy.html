Privacy Policy

This page describes the basic privacy approach for the current SMCyvera site. It should be updated if analytics, advertising, contact services, or other third-party features are added.

Information you provide

Public guides and tools do not require an account. If you contact the site owner, the information you choose to include—such as your name, email address, and message—may be received by the site owner for the purpose of responding.

Cookies and advertising

If advertising or analytics are enabled, the site may use cookies or similar technologies supplied by those services. The policy should be updated with the exact services used, the information they collect, and the choices available to visitors before those services are activated.

Third-party services

Third-party services can have their own privacy policies and data practices. Visitors should review those policies when using an external service linked from this site.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
