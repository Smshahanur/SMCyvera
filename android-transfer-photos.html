How to Transfer Photos From Android to PC

By Shahanur Mollah · Published September 2026 · Updated October 2026
Quick answerFor a dependable Android-to-PC photo transfer, use a USB cable with data support, unlock the phone, select the appropriate USB file-transfer option, and copy the photos to a clearly named folder on the PC before deleting anything from the phone.

USB transfer checklist

  • Connect the phone with a known data-capable cable and unlock it. Some charging-only cables cannot transfer files.
  • On the phone's USB notification, choose a file-transfer option when available instead of charge-only mode.
  • On the PC, open the phone's storage and locate the camera/photo folders. Copy first; verify the files on the PC before deleting originals.
  • For a large transfer, keep the phone awake if required by the device and avoid disconnecting the cable while files are being copied.

USB cable

Connect the phone with a data-capable USB cable, unlock it, and select the appropriate USB file-transfer option if Android asks. Some cables are charge-only.

Cloud transfer

A cloud photo service can synchronize photos to a PC without a cable. Confirm the originals are backed up before deleting anything from the phone.

Wireless transfer

Nearby-device and local-network transfer options can work well for smaller batches. Use trusted software and keep both devices on the same trusted network when required.

Keep your originals organized

Create a dated folder structure on the PC and verify several transferred files before deleting the phone copies.

If USB does not appear

Try another cable or USB port, unlock the phone, change the USB mode, and check whether the computer detects the device at all.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Disconnecting the cable while files are still copying.
  • Selecting the wrong USB mode, such as charge-only.
  • Moving files instead of copying them before verifying the transfer.
  • Assuming every cable supports data just because it can charge the phone.

Questions to check before you finish

Why does my phone only charge when connected to a PC?

The USB connection may be in charge-only mode, or the cable may not support data. Unlock the phone and check the USB connection notification.

Should I copy or move photos first?

Copy them first, verify the files on the PC, and only then consider deleting the originals. This reduces the risk of losing photos during a failed transfer.

Can any USB-C cable transfer photos?

No. The connector shape does not guarantee data capability. Some cables are intended mainly for charging.
